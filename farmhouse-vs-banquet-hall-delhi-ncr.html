Farmhouse vs Banquet Hall in Delhi NCR

COMPARE THE TRADE-OFFS

Check the practical differences before choosing.

Use these points as a comparison framework. Actual facilities, rules and package inclusions vary by property and must be confirmed directly.

01

Space and event layout

Ask which areas are reserved for your event and whether ceremony, dining and entertainment can happen in separate spaces. Confirm layout-specific capacity, guest circulation, washrooms and service access rather than relying on a headline capacity.

02

Facilities and service responsibility

Clarify what the venue provides and what you must arrange separately, such as catering, furniture, décor, power backup, cleaning, security, lighting or staff. Ask who coordinates vendors and who is responsible for each part of the event.

03

Access, parking and neighbours

Check the exact approach route, parking arrangements, accessibility, vendor loading, sound limits and event end time. For a property with outdoor space, ask about lighting, safe routes and how guests move between areas after dark.

04

Weather and contingency

Ask what happens if rain, heat or wind affects an outdoor setup. Inspect the actual alternative space, confirm the same guest layout can fit and get the trigger, responsibilities and additional charges in writing.

05

Total cost and terms

Compare itemised quotes covering venue rental, food, applicable taxes, rentals, décor, power, staffing, deposits, overtime, outside-vendor charges and cancellation terms. A lower base rental may still require more separate arrangements.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

Is a farmhouse always more private than a banquet hall?

Not necessarily. Ask which areas are exclusively reserved, whether other events can use shared spaces and what access or sound restrictions apply.

Does a farmhouse package include catering and furniture?

Packages vary. Get an itemised written list of included services, rentals, vendor permissions and additional charges.

What should I compare before booking a farmhouse or banquet hall?

Compare actual layout, capacity, facilities, weather backup, guest access, service responsibility, full costs and written terms.
