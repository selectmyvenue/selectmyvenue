BEFORE YOU COMPARE QUOTES

Ask every venue for the same cost breakdown.

Keep the answers side by side. If a charge is unclear, ask whether it is included, optional or payable separately.

01

Venue and food

Confirm rental charges, minimum guest count, menu options, meal service, drinking water, tasting arrangements and the final headcount deadline.

02

Taxes and extras

Ask for a written estimate showing applicable taxes, service or administrative charges, electricity, generator, cleaning and security deposits.

03

Décor and schedule

Check what décor is included, outside-vendor rules, setup access, sound restrictions, event end time and any overtime charges.

QUOTE COMPARISON

Use this checklist at every venue visit.

1. What exactly is included in the package?

Request an itemised quote. Separate the venue, food, furniture, basic lighting, décor, sound, valet or parking, rooms and any outside-vendor fees. Do not assume that a feature shown in photographs is included in the quoted package.

2. What can change the final bill?

Ask about minimum guarantees, extra guests, menu upgrades, overtime, additional power, damage deposits, cancellation or date-change terms, and when each payment is due. Get important answers in writing before paying an advance.

3. Does the venue suit the event logistics?

Check guest travel, parking capacity, accessibility, weather contingency for outdoor areas, washrooms, power backup, vendor loading access and a practical plan for elderly guests or children.

4. How should I compare two different formats?

Compare the same guest count and event duration. A lawn, farmhouse, hotel and banquet hall may package services differently, so compare the complete list of included services and exclusions rather than only the rental or per-person figure.

5. What should be confirmed before booking?

Confirm the event date and time, payment schedule, refund or cancellation conditions, final guest-count deadline, vendor permissions, setup and teardown windows, and the person responsible for coordinating on event day.
