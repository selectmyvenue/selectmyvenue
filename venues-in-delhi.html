DELHI SEARCH PRIORITIES

Make location part of the guest experience.

A venue that looks nearby on a map can still be difficult during peak traffic. Compare guest origins, public transport, road access, parking and late-night travel before finalising a locality.

01

Travel radius

Choose a practical zone based on where most guests will travel from and when the event ends.

02

Parking reality

Confirm on-site parking, valet capacity, bus access and overflow arrangements for the actual guest count.

03

Venue fit

Compare indoor and outdoor capacity, catering, décor rules, rooms and weather backup together.
