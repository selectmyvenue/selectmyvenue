Corporate Event Venue Checklist for Delhi NCR

A corporate venue must support the event's working requirements as well as the guest experience. Before confirming a meeting, conference, team event or company celebration, check the layout, technology, service plan and commercial terms.

PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for each venue so that the comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Define the event format

Clarify whether you need a boardroom, classroom, theatre-style setup, networking space, meal service or a mix of formats. Share the agenda, timings, guest count and room-change requirements before requesting a quote.

02

Check AV, power and connectivity

Confirm the actual microphones, screens, projectors, speakers, adapters, Wi-Fi and technical support included. Ask whether a rehearsal or setup window is available and who will help if equipment fails.

03

Review catering and breaks

Specify arrival refreshments, working lunch, tea breaks, dietary needs and service timing. Ask whether food can be served without disrupting sessions and how changes in attendance are handled.

04

Confirm access and operations

Check registration space, signage, accessibility, parking or transport, loading access, power backup, security requirements and the named venue coordinator. Confirm the event end time and overtime charges.

05

Get commercial terms in writing

Ask for an itemised quote, applicable taxes, deposit and payment milestones, cancellation terms, invoice details, vendor rules and the process for approving additional costs.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What should I check before booking a corporate event venue?

Confirm layout, guest count, AV and connectivity, catering, access, timings, power backup, invoice requirements and written payment/cancellation terms.

Should AV equipment be included in the venue quote?

Do not assume it is included. Ask for an itemised list of equipment, technical support, setup time and additional charges.

What information should I give a venue for a corporate quote?

Share the date, agenda, guest count, room layout, AV needs, meal schedule, accessibility needs and invoice details.
