Questions to Ask Before Booking a Wedding Venue in Delhi NCR

Before paying an advance, make sure the date, package, responsibilities and cancellation terms are clear. Use this checklist to compare written offers from banquet halls, hotels, lawns and farmhouses without assuming that every venue follows the same booking policy.

BEFORE YOU PAY

Confirm the booking essentials in writing.

Ask the same questions of every shortlisted venue so you can compare offers fairly. A written response is more useful than an assumption based on photographs or a verbal conversation.

01

Date and time hold

Ask whether your date is currently available, how long a tentative hold lasts, when it becomes a confirmed booking and what happens if another customer enquires for the same date.

02

Advance and payment milestones

Confirm the amount due now, each later payment deadline, acceptable payment methods, receipts and the point at which the booking is considered confirmed.

03

Cancellation and date changes

Read the written refund, cancellation, rescheduling and postponement terms. Ask how the policy changes as the event date approaches; do not assume the advance is refundable.

CHECK THE SCOPE

Make package inclusions and exclusions specific.

What spaces and services are reserved?

List the exact hall, lawn, rooms or other areas included, the event timings, setup and teardown windows, guest-count basis and whether another event can use shared spaces at the same time.

What is included in the quoted amount?

Ask for an itemised list covering venue rental, food and beverage, furniture, basic lighting, décor, sound, cleaning, security, parking or valet, power backup and applicable taxes or extra charges. Mark every exclusion clearly.

Which outside vendors are allowed?

Confirm policies for decorators, caterers, photographers, DJs, planners and other vendors. Ask about vendor entry times, loading access, outside-vendor fees, insurance or security requirements and restrictions.

How are guest-count changes handled?

Ask when the final headcount is due, how extra guests are charged, whether a minimum guarantee applies and what happens if attendance is lower than expected.

Who is responsible on the event day?

Get the name and contact method of the venue coordinator, escalation contact and the person who can approve changes. Confirm the agreed event end time, overtime rules and how any last-minute costs are authorised.

What should I receive after paying?

Keep the receipt, booking confirmation, accepted quote, terms and any written clarifications together. Check that the date, venue spaces, amount, payment milestones and named customer details are correct.
