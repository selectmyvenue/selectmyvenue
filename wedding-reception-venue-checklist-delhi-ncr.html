PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for each venue so that the comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Plan guest arrival and movement

Ask how guests enter, where they wait, how greeting will work and whether routes to the stage, dining area and washrooms stay clear when the venue is busy.

02

Check stage and seating visibility

Review stage position, lighting, sound, seating layout, photography angles and space for any planned performance or dance floor. Ask for a layout based on your expected guest count.

03

Confirm food and event timing

Ask about service format, menu inclusions, final guest-count deadlines, event end time, sound restrictions and charges for additional time or guests.

04

Check logistics and facilities

Confirm parking, accessibility, washrooms, power backup, vendor loading access and who will coordinate during the event. If outdoor space is involved, inspect the backup arrangement.

05

Compare package and terms

Request an itemised quote covering venue rental, food, applicable taxes, décor, lighting, power, staffing, deposits and extras. Review payment milestones, cancellation rules and vendor permissions.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What matters most when choosing a reception venue?

Check guest flow, stage visibility, dining layout, sound and timing restrictions, parking, accessibility, package inclusions and written terms.

Should the reception guest count determine the layout?

Yes. Ask for a layout designed for the expected guest count and intended stage, dining and circulation requirements, not only maximum capacity.

What should be in the reception venue quote?

Ask for venue hire, food, taxes, décor, lighting, staffing, power, extra-guest rates, overtime, deposits and outside-vendor charges.
