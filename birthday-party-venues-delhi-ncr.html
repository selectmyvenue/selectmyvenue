BIRTHDAY VENUE FAQ

Common questions

What venue type is suitable for a birthday?

Party halls, restaurants, banquet halls, hotels and event spaces can work depending on guest count, age group, food, entertainment and budget.

How do I avoid booking a venue that is too large?

Use a realistic guest count and compare usable seating, dining and activity space rather than only advertised maximum capacity.
