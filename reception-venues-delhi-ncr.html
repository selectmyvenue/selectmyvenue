RECEPTION VENUE FAQ

Common questions

What should I compare in a reception venue?

Compare usable capacity, stage and dining layout, food package, parking, décor policy, music timing, rooms and total cost.

Are banquet halls or lawns better?

It depends on season, guest count and event style. Banquets offer controlled indoor comfort; lawns provide flexible outdoor layouts when weather permits.
