BEFORE A SITE VISIT

What to confirm with every wedding venue

  • Usable capacity: confirm seating, stage, dance floor and dining layout—not only the advertised maximum.
  • Commercial terms: compare food package, venue rental, décor rules, taxes, minimum guarantee and overtime.
  • Guest convenience: check parking, valet, accessibility, nearby accommodation and travel time.
  • Operations: ask about vendor entry, music timing, backup power, weather backup and cancellation terms.

WEDDING VENUE FAQ

Common planning questions

How do I shortlist a wedding venue in Delhi NCR?

Begin with location, date, realistic guest count and budget. Compare capacity, catering, parking, rooms and ceremony needs before visiting.

Which venue types can I consider?

Banquet halls, hotels, lawns, resorts, farmhouses and smaller celebration spaces can all work depending on the event format and date availability.

Can I search Gurgaon and Noida too?
