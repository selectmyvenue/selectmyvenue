USEFUL BEFORE YOU BOOK

Three things that save time during wedding venue shortlisting.

Use these checks before visiting properties so you compare practical fit, not only photographs.

01

Usable capacity

Ask for seating, stage, dining and movement capacity—not only the advertised maximum.

02

Total commercial cost

Compare menu, rental, décor rules, taxes, minimum guarantee and overtime together.

03

Guest convenience

Parking, rooms, accessibility and travel time can matter as much as décor.
