WEDDING LAWN FAQ

Common questions

What should I check before booking a wedding lawn?

Check usable capacity, weather backup, parking, washrooms, catering and vendor rules, power backup, music timing and guest access.

Do I need indoor backup?

A covered or indoor backup is worth checking for rain, heat, cold or wind, especially for seasonal outdoor events.
