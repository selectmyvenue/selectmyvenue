Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

About Select My Venue

Select My Venue is an Udyam-registered MSME operating as a sole proprietorship in Delhi, India. We provide venue discovery, enquiry matching and related digital services for customers and venue partners.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, city or location, event type, event date, guest count, budget, food preferences, requirements and messages you send to us. For venue partners, we may also collect venue, business contact, listing and account information needed to provide partner services.

How we use information

We use personal information to receive and respond to enquiries, identify and recommend potentially suitable venues, share relevant requirements with selected venue partners, operate our CRM and partner services, communicate about an enquiry, maintain platform security, prevent misuse, improve our services and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing with venue partners and service providers

Relevant enquiry details may be shared with selected venue partners when reasonably needed to help fulfil your venue request. Venue partners are expected to use assigned customer information only for the relevant enquiry. We may also use technology and service providers that help us host, secure, operate and communicate through the platform. We do not sell personal information.

AI-assisted and smart matching

Select My Venue may use automated rules or AI-assisted features to organise requirements, identify potentially relevant venues or provide planning assistance. These features are intended to assist discovery and do not guarantee venue availability, suitability, pricing or booking.

Security and retention

We use access controls and technical measures designed to protect information and limit access to authorised purposes. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, operational, security or legal purposes for which it is processed.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of eligible personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or opt out of marketing communications. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out and may limit services that require the relevant information.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Select My Venue develops, introduces new features or as applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
