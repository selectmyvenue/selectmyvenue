Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or venue partner application, we may collect your name, contact details, event requirements, venue information and messages you send to us.

We use this information to respond to requests, identify suitable venues, operate our CRM and partner services, improve our service, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

Relevant enquiry details may be shared with selected venue partners only when needed to help with your request. Partners are expected to use this information only for the assigned enquiry. We do not sell personal information.

We use authenticated systems and access controls designed to limit data access. Information is retained only as reasonably needed for service, operational or legal purposes.
