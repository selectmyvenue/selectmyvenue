WEDDING PLANNING · DELHI NCR

How to Shortlist Wedding Venues in Delhi NCR

A good shortlist is not the longest list. It is a manageable set of venues that fit your date, guest count, location, budget and event priorities—and whose important details you can verify before booking.

START YOUR SHORTLIST

Share your wedding requirements

Provide the basics so your search can focus on relevant venue options. Confirm availability and package details with each venue.

STEP 1 · DEFINE THE BRIEF

Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves.

Write down what would make a venue unsuitable before comparing photographs or packages. This keeps the shortlist practical and makes enquiries easier to evaluate.

01

Date and timing

Record the preferred date, backup dates if possible, event duration and setup time. A venue should not be treated as available until the venue confirms it.

02

Guest count and format

Estimate attendance and decide whether you need a banquet hall, hotel space, lawn, farmhouse or another format. Ask how the venue handles seated dining and guest movement.

03

Location and travel

Choose a realistic area based on guest travel, parking and transport. If the area is flexible, list preferred areas in order rather than treating all of Delhi NCR as interchangeable.

STEP 2 · FILTER FAIRLY

Use the same criteria for every option.

Can the venue accommodate the event?

Ask for the suitable capacity for your intended layout, not only a maximum number shown in a listing. Confirm seating, dining, stage or dance-floor requirements and whether the proposed spaces are exclusive to your event.

Does the full cost fit?

Compare itemised written quotes for the same guest count and event duration. Include venue rental, food, applicable taxes, décor, power, rooms, parking, overtime and any required deposits or outside-vendor charges.

Are the essential facilities confirmed?

Check each must-have—such as parking, rooms, accessibility, power backup, indoor weather backup or a preferred food arrangement—with the venue directly. Do not assume a facility is available because it appears in an old photo or an unverified listing.

Are the terms workable?

Ask about tentative date holds, advance payments, cancellation and rescheduling, final guest-count deadlines, vendor permissions and setup or teardown windows. Keep written responses with the quote.

STEP 3 · VISIT AND VERIFY

Visit the strongest matches, then compare notes.

Use one checklist at each visit. Record what was confirmed, what remains unclear and who will provide the missing information. Do not make a final choice from photos alone.
