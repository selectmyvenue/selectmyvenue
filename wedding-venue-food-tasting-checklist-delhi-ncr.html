Wedding Venue Food Tasting Checklist for Delhi NCR

PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for every shortlisted venue so that your comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Agree on the tasting scope

Ask whether a tasting is available, whether it is complimentary or chargeable, how many people can attend and which dishes can be sampled. Confirm whether the tasting menu represents the proposed event menu and whether changes affect the price.

02

Compare the menu consistently

Use the same meal format and approximate menu scope when comparing venues. Discuss starters, main dishes, breads, desserts, beverages and service timing where relevant. Ask how substitutions or seasonal ingredients are handled and when the final menu must be confirmed.

03

Check dietary and guest requirements

Tell the venue about vegetarian and non-vegetarian preferences, allergies and other dietary requirements that matter to your guests. Ask how the kitchen will avoid mix-ups and how special requests are documented. Do not assume a preference can be accommodated without confirmation.

04

Ask about service at event scale

Taste alone does not show how a large service will run. Ask about buffet replenishment, service staff, food holding, water and beverage service, meal timing and how the venue handles changes in the final guest count.

05

Clarify food-related costs

Confirm the number of menu items, package inclusions, upgrades, tasting charges, extra-guest rates, applicable taxes, outside catering rules and any beverage or service restrictions. Ask for the final menu and price to be attached to the booking documents.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What should I ask during a wedding venue food tasting?

Ask which dishes are included, whether the tasting reflects the event menu, how substitutions are priced, how dietary needs are handled and what food-service charges are additional.

Does a tasting guarantee the same food on the event day?

A tasting helps you evaluate the proposed menu, but confirm the final menu, preparation expectations, service timing and substitutions in writing.

Can I change the menu after booking?

Policies vary. Ask the venue when menu changes must be finalised and whether substitutions, upgrades or changes in guest count affect the price.
