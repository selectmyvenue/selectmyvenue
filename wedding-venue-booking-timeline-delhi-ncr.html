PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for every shortlisted venue so that your comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Step 1: Define the brief before searching

Write down the preferred date or date range, expected guest count, target areas, event format, budget and non-negotiable facilities. Decide which preferences can change. A clear brief helps you compare venues on the same basis.

02

Step 2: Check dates and shortlist options

Ask venues to confirm whether the requested date is available and whether any hold is tentative or binding. Availability can change, so treat a listing or earlier conversation as a starting point rather than a booking confirmation.

03

Step 3: Arrange site visits

Visit the strongest options and inspect the event spaces, guest flow, washrooms, access, parking, power backup and indoor or outdoor contingency. Ask who will coordinate on the event day and what is included.

04

Step 4: Compare complete written quotes

Use the same guest count, timing and requirements for each venue. Review the itemised cost, applicable taxes, deposits, payment schedule, extra-guest rules, cancellation and rescheduling conditions, vendor permissions and setup access.

05

Step 5: Confirm before paying

Read the final booking document carefully. Make sure the event date, reserved spaces, amount, inclusions, payment milestones and cancellation terms match the accepted quote. Obtain a receipt and keep written clarifications.

06

Step 6: Reconfirm closer to the event

Reconfirm the final guest count, menu, vendors, access times, layout, point of contact and agreed changes. Keep a record of approvals so the venue team and event organiser work from the same plan.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

How early should I book a wedding venue?

There is no single timeline that fits every event. Start once your date, guest count and preferred format are clear, then ask venues about availability and deadlines.

Does a tentative date hold mean the venue is booked?

Not necessarily. Ask how long the hold lasts, what conditions apply and what payment or written confirmation turns it into a confirmed booking.

What should I finalise before paying an advance?

Confirm the date, spaces, full quote, inclusions, payment milestones, cancellation or rescheduling terms and written booking confirmation.
