AFFORDABLE VENUE FAQ

Common questions

How can I compare affordable venues fairly?

Compare food, rental, taxes, minimum guarantee, décor rules, parking, rooms, overtime and mandatory services as one complete estimate.

Does the lowest per-person price mean the lowest total cost?

No. Other charges can change the total significantly, so compare written packages and what each one includes.
