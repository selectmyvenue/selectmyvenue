How to compare these options

  1. Confirm the event date and the exact space available for your guest count.
  2. Request an itemised quote, including food, venue rental, applicable taxes, décor, power, parking and any additional charges.
  3. Check guest movement, accessibility, facilities, setup and teardown times, and outside-vendor rules.
  4. Read payment milestones, cancellation and rescheduling terms before paying an advance.
  5. Visit the strongest matches and get important promises in writing.
