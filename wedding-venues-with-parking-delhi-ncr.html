PARKING FAQ

Common questions

What should I ask a venue about parking?

Ask about on-site capacity, valet, overflow parking, coach access, drop-off space, security and separate parking charges.

How much parking does a wedding need?

Estimate likely cars, taxis and buses from your guest travel plan instead of assuming every guest will arrive separately.
