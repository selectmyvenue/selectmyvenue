Wedding Venue Budget Guide for Delhi NCR

By Select My Venue · Updated 17 September 2026

The advertised per-person rate is only one part of a celebration budget. Compare written packages for your date and guest count, with the same food, spaces and services included.

Start with the guest commitment

Ask for both the expected attendance and the minimum number of guests you must pay for. If a venue requires a minimum of 200 guests, a lower final attendance may not reduce the bill.

Illustrative calculation—not a market quote

200 guests × ₹1,500 per person = ₹3,00,000 for the quoted per-person component. Add any separately quoted taxes, rental, décor, rooms or services. Actual charges depend on the venue and written package.

Ask what the package includes

Cost to checkQuestion to ask
Food and beveragesWhich menu, service duration and extra plate rate are included?
Venue spaceIs rental included? Which halls, lawns and changing rooms can we use?
Décor and vendorsWhat is included, and can we use our own vendors?
Taxes and chargesCan you give us the complete payable amount in writing?
Rooms and parkingAre these included, limited or separately charged?
Changes and cancellationWhat happens if our date, guest count or plans change?

Choose your priorities before upgrading

Decide what matters most: convenient travel, food, décor, overnight rooms or an outdoor setting. Compare options against those priorities, and keep an allowance for items that are not yet quoted.

Use starting prices to shortlist, not to book

Our venue directory can filter published starting prices. Treat this as a discovery tool, then ask for a date-specific quote and review the venue's written terms.
