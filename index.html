How do I find a venue on Select My Venue?

Start with your city, guest count and per-person budget, then browse the live venue directory. Open a venue profile to see its published details, or share your requirements for personalised help.

Can I compare and save venues?

Yes. Use Shortlist on venue cards to save favourites in this browser, and Compare to see up to three venues side by side. These saved choices are local to your browser and are not an account-based booking.

Are the prices and dates guaranteed?

Published prices are starting points, not final quotes. Availability, taxes, minimum guest commitments, menus and inclusions must be confirmed with the venue team before you book.

Can you help with weddings, birthdays and corporate events?

Yes. Share the occasion and requirements with our team. Suitability depends on the venue, guest count, layout and date; a listing does not guarantee that every event can be hosted.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

Your requirements go to the Select My Venue team for follow-up about suitable options. Submitting an enquiry does not reserve a venue or complete a booking.

What should I check before paying a booking advance?

Visit the venue where possible and ask for a written package, date and time slot, guest commitment, all applicable charges, payment schedule and cancellation or rescheduling terms.
