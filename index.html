ABOUT SELECT MY VENUE

Finding the right venue should be exciting — not exhausting.

Select My Venue is built around one simple idea: finding the right venue should be easier for the customer. We combine requirement-led discovery, smart planning and human support to make your venue search simpler, faster and more relevant. Whether you're planning a wedding, birthday, engagement, corporate event or a special celebration, we help you start with what you actually need rather than making you scroll through endless venue listings.

Share your location, date, guest count, budget and preferences. Select My Venue turns those details into a clearer starting point for your venue search — and as our venue network grows, you'll have more relevant options to explore.
