PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for each venue so that the comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Match the venue to the party plan

Clarify whether the event is for children, adults or a mixed group. List expected guests, activities, seating, cake cutting, entertainment and the time needed for setup and cleanup.

02

Confirm food and decoration rules

Ask what food or menu packages are available, whether outside cake or catering is permitted, what decoration is included and whether outside decorators or entertainers incur fees.

03

Check sound, timing and safety

Confirm music restrictions, end time, supervision expectations for children's activities, washroom access, safe circulation and any venue rules for games or equipment.

04

Compare the full price

Ask whether the quote includes venue hire, food, taxes, décor, staff, cleaning, power, extra guests and overtime. Check minimum guest commitments and how the final headcount is handled.

05

Plan the guest experience

Consider travel, parking, accessibility, waiting space and weather backup if activities are outdoors. Confirm the event-day contact and keep agreed terms in writing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What should I ask before booking a birthday venue?

Confirm the date, guest count, food, decoration rules, music limits, setup and cleanup times, full price and cancellation terms.

Can I bring my own cake or decorator?

Policies vary. Ask about outside items, vendor permissions, handling requirements and extra fees before booking.

How do I compare birthday party packages?

Compare the same guest count and duration, then list food, venue, décor, staff, taxes, extra-guest rates and restrictions.
