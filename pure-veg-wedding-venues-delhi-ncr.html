Common questions

What does pure veg venue mean?

Policies vary. Confirm whether the venue is exclusively vegetarian, whether outside catering is permitted and whether kitchen or service areas meet your specific requirements.

Can I request Jain or specialised vegetarian menus?

Yes, include the requirement in your enquiry and confirm exact preparation arrangements with shortlisted venues before booking.
