PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for each venue so that the comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Clarify the ceremony format

Decide whether you need a ring ceremony area, stage, seating for close family, a larger gathering or a meal after the ceremony. Share the run-of-show and expected guest count.

02

Check stage, seating and photography

Confirm sightlines, stage access, lighting, photography positions, guest movement and whether décor can be placed without blocking aisles or service routes.

03

Compare food and service options

Ask about the proposed menu, tasting options, meal timing, dietary needs, beverages and how service will work during the ceremony. Confirm whether staff and equipment are included.

04

Review timing and vendor rules

Check setup and teardown windows, music or sound limits, outside decorator and photographer permissions, parking and charges for additional time or production.

05

Confirm the booking details

Get the date, reserved spaces, package inclusions, full price, payment schedule, cancellation conditions and event-day contact in writing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What should I prioritise when choosing an engagement venue?

Prioritise ceremony layout, guest count, stage and photography needs, food service, timing, access and the full written package.

Can an engagement venue also host a meal or reception?

Some venues may support multiple functions, but confirm spaces, timings, service transitions and costs for each part of the event.

What should be confirmed before paying an engagement venue advance?

Confirm the date, spaces, full quote, inclusions, payment schedule, cancellation terms and written booking confirmation.
