PRACTICAL CHECKLIST

Work through these checks before deciding.

Use the same checklist for every shortlisted venue so that your comparison is based on confirmed details rather than assumptions.

01

Start with the full event cost

Ask for the complete amount for your expected guest count and event duration. Separate venue rental, food and beverage, applicable taxes, décor, furniture, lighting, power backup, cleaning, security, parking or valet, rooms and any required deposits. A per-person quote may not include every venue or production charge.

02

Ask about charges that appear later

Confirm extra-guest rates, minimum guarantees, menu upgrades, overtime, setup or teardown access, outside-vendor fees, generator or electricity charges, damage deposits and cancellation or date-change costs. Ask which charges are mandatory, optional or conditional.

03

Compare written quotes on the same basis

Give every venue the same guest estimate, event timing, meal format and must-have facilities. Ask each venue to state the quote's validity period, payment milestones and the deadline for confirming final guest numbers. Do not compare a base package with an all-inclusive estimate as though they were equivalent.

04

Use a simple cost review

Create columns for included, excluded, optional, amount and written confirmation. If a venue cannot confirm a figure yet, mark it as pending rather than treating it as zero. Keep the latest quote and clarifications together.

05

Before paying an advance

Confirm the event date, reserved spaces, package total, applicable taxes, refund or cancellation terms, vendor rules and payment schedule in writing. Ask for a receipt for every payment and keep a copy of the accepted quote.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions to clarify with the venue.

What extra charges should I ask a wedding venue about?

Ask about applicable taxes, décor, power backup, overtime, setup access, extra guests, parking, rooms, outside-vendor fees, deposits and cancellation or date-change terms. The exact list depends on the venue and package.

Is price per plate the final wedding venue cost?

Not necessarily. Confirm whether the quote includes venue rental, taxes, décor, production, power, parking and any minimum guarantee or additional charge.

How do I compare two venue quotes fairly?

Use the same guest count, event duration, meal format and facilities, then compare itemised inclusions, exclusions, payment milestones and cancellation terms in writing.
