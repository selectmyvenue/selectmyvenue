Wedding Venue Site-Visit Checklist for Delhi NCR

Photos and a package quote cannot answer every practical question. Use this checklist during venue visits to assess whether the space works for your guests, schedule and event plan.

DURING THE WALK-THROUGH

Check the guest experience, not only the décor.

Walk the route a guest would take from arrival to departure, and ask the venue representative to explain how the event will run at your expected guest count.

01

Arrival and circulation

Check the approach road, drop-off point, parking arrangement, entry queues, lift or stair access and how guests will move between spaces.

02

Event setup

Ask where the stage, dining, ceremony, photography, vendor loading and guest seating will fit. Confirm what can be changed and what is fixed.

03

Comfort and contingency

Inspect washrooms, ventilation or cooling, power backup, accessibility, weather backup for outdoor spaces and a suitable waiting area.

TAKE THIS CHECKLIST WITH YOU

Questions to answer before you leave.

Space, capacity and layout

Ask for the usable seating and dining layouts for your expected attendance. Check whether ceremony, buffet, stage and circulation areas compete for the same space. If multiple events can run at once, ask how sound and guest movement are separated.

Food, service and vendor access

Confirm menu options, tasting availability, service style, outside caterer rules, kitchen access, vendor arrival windows and the deadline for the final guest count. Clarify whether staff, furniture, basic lighting and cleanup are included.

Access, parking and facilities

Check parking capacity and whether it is reserved or shared. Look at washroom condition, step-free access where needed, lifts, power backup, mobile connectivity and practical arrangements for older guests and children.

Weather, noise and timing

For outdoor or mixed spaces, ask what the rain or extreme-weather plan actually is and whether it is available for your date. Confirm event end times, sound limits, setup and teardown access, and any overtime charges.

Commercial terms and evidence

Request an itemised written quote, payment milestones, cancellation and date-change terms, refundable deposits, additional-charge rules and the name of your event-day contact. Take notes and, where permitted, photos of the spaces you are comparing.
